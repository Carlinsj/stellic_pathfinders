# NYU recreation facility catalog

CampusFit's NYU tenant models the four member athletic facilities listed by NYU Athletics. Activity availability remains schedule-dependent; an activity in this catalog means that the facility has the relevant space or resource, not that it is continuously open for free play.

## Palladium Athletic Facility

- Address: 140 E 14th St
- Activities: swimming, basketball, volleyball, climbing, cycling, and group fitness
- Verified resources: eight-lane pool, one multi-use court, and five climbing stations
- Source: https://gonyuathletics.com/sports/2024/1/28/palladium-athletic-facility.aspx

## John A. Paulson Center

- Address: 181 Mercer St
- Activities: swimming, basketball, volleyball, badminton, pickleball, squash, indoor track, and group fitness
- Verified resources: six-lane pool, four multi-use courts, and two squash courts
- Source: https://gonyuathletics.com/sports/2024/1/17/paulson-center.aspx

## 404 Fitness

- Address: 404 Lafayette St
- Activities: group fitness, cycling, and functional training
- Verified resources: two fitness studios and a functional-training turf area
- Source: https://gonyuathletics.com/sports/2024/1/28/404-fitness-facility.aspx

## Brooklyn Athletic Facility

- Address: 6 MetroTech Center
- Activities: basketball, volleyball, badminton, futsal, table tennis, cricket, and group fitness
- Verified resource: one regulation-sized multi-use court; other listed court activities are scheduled uses of the gymnasium
- Source: https://gonyuathletics.com/sports/2014/10/8/BAF_1008141433.aspx

Daily hours, closures, and activity-area schedules must be checked separately:
